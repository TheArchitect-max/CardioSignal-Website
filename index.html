Why it exists

ECG development becomes difficult to trust when signal, data, model, and evaluation evidence drift apart.

CardioSignal is designed to keep those stages connected at a high level: what signal was used, which evidence governed it, which model-development path was followed, and how evaluation results relate back to the selected candidate.

The goal is not to replace scientific or clinical judgment. The goal is to provide a disciplined engineering layer for ECG research workflows where provenance, repeatability, and explicit boundaries matter.

Design principles

Technical discipline without turning the public site into a technical blueprint.

Traceability

Know what evidence belongs to which step.

Product contracts are designed to retain context instead of reconstructing it after the fact.

Separation

Keep development and final evaluation distinct.

Training and validation decisions are treated separately from the final test path.

Reproducibility

Prefer repeatable engineering workflows.

Deterministic reference paths and explicit runtime evidence make results easier to inspect and reproduce.

Public restraint

Explain the product, not the proprietary mechanism.

The public website intentionally omits private algorithms, source code, model weights, internal schemas, and sensitive infrastructure.
